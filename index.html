Employee & Customer Experience | Public Opinion & Behavioural Science

I'm a research scientist and Senior Consultant at Gallup, based in Sydney. I lead research on employee and customer experience, organisational culture and public opinion, and work with organisations to understand what the findings mean for their decisions.

My experience combines applied consulting with academic research in social and behavioural science. Across that work, I design and lead quantitative, qualitative and mixed-methods research, from framing the questions and developing the approach to conducting the analysis and interpreting the findings.

Local Government at a Turning Point

Evidence and Insights From Councils Across Australia and New Zealand

Gallup, 2026 | Lead author

This report examines the financial, workforce and community pressures facing local government. It combines Gallup survey data with interviews with 33 council leaders to identify priorities for employee experience, organisational culture and public service.

Singapore Workplace Report 2026

Powering Singapore's Future: The Case for a National Engagement Infrastructure

Gallup and Singapore Institute of Directors, 2026 | Research and writing

This report examines employee engagement, leadership and workplace culture in Singapore. It brings together Gallup survey data, interviews with 16 senior leaders and a leadership roundtable to identify priorities for managers, employee experience and workforce development.

Academic & professional

Background

Before joining Gallup, I was the Howard Deshong Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Pennsylvania from 2023 to 2025, where I studied how communication and social context shape attitudes and decision-making. I was also a Visiting Research Fellow at the Australian National University from 2020 to 2023 and a Visiting Assistant Professor at Michigan State University from 2018 to 2021.

I hold a PhD in Political Science from Michigan State University, and an MA and BA from Tel Aviv University.
