Selected Client Work

These are the main areas of my work at Gallup, followed by examples of projects I have led. Client names are omitted from the project summaries.

Areas of Practice

  • Employee engagement and experience
  • Customer engagement and experience
  • Market research
  • Talent assessment and selection
  • Learning and development
  • Public opinion and social research

Selected Projects

My projects also cover risk and compliance culture, psychosocial hazards, gender equity action plans, business impact analysis and voice of the customer research.

01

Designing an organisational culture assessment

I designed and validated a compliance-culture assessment that an organisation now uses to monitor culture risk. The findings were reported to senior leadership.

02

Redesigning a customer research programme

I directed the research strategy for an established customer research programme, guiding the team in using historical customer data to redesign its approach. The work included developing a strategy to improve survey participation, with findings presented to the client.

03

Research for a Gender Equity Action Plan

I led the full engagement for an organisation-wide Gender Equity Action Plan, from winning the work through research design and delivery to the final report.

04

Connecting engagement with business performance

I analysed the relationships between employee engagement, retention, customer satisfaction and sales performance. I brought these findings together to inform senior leaders' priorities across the organisation and its business units.

05

Voice of the customer research

I designed and conducted interviews with senior stakeholders to understand how they valued an organisation's partnerships. The research assessed those relationships and identified priorities for future collaboration.
