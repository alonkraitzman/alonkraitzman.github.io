Research Reports

My public reports at Gallup examine employee experience, leadership and the pressures facing local government.

01 / REPORT

Local Government at a Turning Point

Evidence and Insights From Councils Across Australia and New Zealand

Gallup, 2026 | Lead author

This report examines the financial, workforce and community pressures facing local government. It combines Gallup survey data with interviews with 33 council leaders to identify priorities for employee experience, organisational culture and public service.

Read the report

02 / REPORT

Singapore Workplace Report 2026

Powering Singapore's Future: The Case for a National Engagement Infrastructure

Gallup and Singapore Institute of Directors, 2026 | Research and writing

This report examines employee engagement, leadership and workplace culture in Singapore. It brings together Gallup survey data, interviews with 16 senior leaders and a leadership roundtable to identify priorities for managers, employee experience and workforce development.
